PROJECT PREPARATION

A LITTLE PREPARATION CAN MAKE A BIG DIFFERENCE.

If you are planning an outdoor project, a few details can help you explain your ideas more clearly.

Photographs of Your Space

Share clear pictures of the existing backyard, access paths, or outdoor area.

Approximate Measurements

Include rough dimensions of length and width to establish spatial scale.

Your Main Idea

Explain whether you are considering a deck, pergola, patio, or complete living area.

Preferred Materials

Mention any timber species, composite decking, natural stone, or finish preferences.

How You Plan to Use the Space

Everyday family relaxation, outdoor dining, weekend entertaining, or garden access.

Questions You Would Like to Ask

List any specific site considerations, shade questions, or details you want to explore.
