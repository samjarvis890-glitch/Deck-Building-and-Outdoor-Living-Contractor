Consider the available area, ground conditions, access from the home, intended use, deck height, material options, drainage, and how the deck will connect with the garden.
A deck is generally an outdoor platform that may be raised or positioned at ground level and is commonly constructed using decking materials. A patio is typically a ground-level outdoor surface that may use stone, concrete, pavers, or other suitable finishes.
Consider its location, dimensions, intended purpose, shade requirements, structural design, materials, and relationship with nearby buildings and landscaping.
A pergola and deck can be considered together as part of one outdoor living design. Their positioning, structural requirements, access, and intended use should be planned together.

MATERIALS & CARE

UNDERSTANDING MATERIALS AND LONG-TERM CARE.

Explore hardwood options, composite decking characteristics, finish durability, and general maintenance.

Common options include natural timber and composite decking products. The appropriate choice depends on appearance, product specifications, installation requirements, exposure, maintenance preferences, and project suitability.
Yes. Natural timber has natural grain and material variation, while composite decking is manufactured using a combination of materials. Compare the specific product specifications, appearance, maintenance requirements, and installation guidance before choosing.
Outdoor timber may require periodic cleaning, inspection, and suitable maintenance depending on the timber type, finish, exposure, and manufacturer's recommendations.
Consider the overall design, material compatibility, exposure to weather, cleaning requirements, surface characteristics, and the intended use of the area.
Follow the product or material manufacturer's guidance. General care may include regular cleaning, removing accumulated debris, checking visible connections and surfaces, and addressing maintenance needs when identified.
There is no single material that is best for every outdoor space. The right option depends on the design, conditions, intended use, product specifications, maintenance preferences, and project requirements.
