Get to Know Evergreen

OUTDOOR LIVING, THOUGHTFULLY IMAGINED.

We believe the spaces beyond your walls deserve the same care and attention as the spaces within them. Evergreen Outdoor Living is centred around thoughtful outdoor design, natural materials, and creating environments that make everyday moments more enjoyable.

“A beautiful outdoor space is not simply an addition to a home. It is an opportunity to enjoy more of the life happening around it.”

What We Believe

A Better Connection Between Home and Outdoors.

We see outdoor spaces as an important part of everyday living. A thoughtfully designed deck, a welcoming pergola, or a comfortable outdoor area can change how people experience their homes. Our philosophy is built around creating spaces that feel natural, practical, and connected to the people who use them.

“Outdoor spaces crafted to enhance everyday residential life through timeless design.”

1. Designed with Purpose

Every outdoor environment should have a clear purpose and suit the way the space is intended to be used.

2. Inspired by Nature

Natural materials, greenery, textures, and light help create an outdoor atmosphere that feels comfortable and welcoming.

3. Made for Everyday Living

A beautiful outdoor space should also be practical, comfortable, and enjoyable to use across every season.

Beyond the Build

It Starts With Understanding How You Live.

Every home, garden, and outdoor area has its own character. Before thinking about materials or structure, it is important to understand what the space means to the people who will enjoy it. We consider the relationship between the home, the available space, natural surroundings, and everyday activities when thinking about an outdoor environment.

INSIGHT 01

The Character of Your Home

Harmonizing architectural lines, roof silhouettes, exterior cladding, and material palettes with your new outdoor environment.

INSIGHT 02

The Possibilities of Your Space

Evaluating natural sunlight patterns, gentle site contours, prevailing breezes, established trees, and spatial flow.

INSIGHT 03

The Way You Enjoy Outdoors

Designing around how you gather — from morning coffee retreats and alfresco dining to family celebrations and quiet evenings.

INSIGHT 04

The Details That Bring It Together

Carefully integrating step heights, flush threshold transitions, subtle illumination, and clean structural alignments.

Our Design Values

Thoughtful Choices Make a Meaningful Difference.

Good outdoor design is about more than appearance. It is about making considered choices that work together to create a comfortable and cohesive environment.

Principle 01

Balance

Bring structure, greenery, textures, and open space together in a natural way.

Principle 02

Function

Consider movement, access, seating, entertaining, and how the area will be used.

Principle 03

Materials

Choose materials and finishes that complement the home and outdoor surroundings.

Principle 04

Detail

Pay attention to proportions, transitions, edges, and finishing touches.

Care in Every Detail

Respect the Material. Refine the Finish.

The character of an outdoor space is shaped by the details. The relationship between surfaces, clean lines, careful material choices, and a considered finish all contribute to the overall result.

Material consistency

Clean and considered details

Thoughtful transitions between spaces

A balanced relationship between structure and surroundings
