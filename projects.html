PLANNING METHODOLOGY

GOOD OUTDOOR SPACES BEGIN WITH GOOD PLANNING.

A successful outdoor design starts with understanding the space. Explore the important considerations that shape an outdoor project before construction begins.

01

Understanding the Site

Consider available space, ground conditions, access, existing structures, and garden layout.

02

Defining the Purpose

Understand how the space should function, whether for dining, relaxing, entertaining, or connecting with the garden.

03

Choosing Materials

Consider suitable materials, appearance, durability, maintenance, and how different finishes work together.

04

Planning the Details

Think about levels, drainage, transitions, lighting considerations, and the relationship between the structure and the surrounding space.

CRAFTSMANSHIP & SPECIFICATION

THE FINER DETAILS MAKE THE DIFFERENCE.

Explore the small design decisions that influence the appearance and functionality of an outdoor space. Material selection, construction details, surface finishes, and clean transitions all contribute to a cohesive result.

Timber Surface and Board Direction

Selecting board orientation and grain patterns to enhance the optical length of the outdoor living terrace.

Structural Lines and Connections

Precision mortise joinery paired with custom steel brackets engineered for durability and architectural clarity.

Natural Stone and Ground Transitions

Seamless junctions between timber fascia and natural dry-stacked fieldstone borders.

Outdoor Lighting and Finishing Touches

Concealed 2700K low-voltage illumination illuminating step risers and defining structural planes after dark.
